# LocalLoop Interactive Prototype — Handmade Neighborhood Marketplace

LocalLoop connects shoppers with home-based makers and micro-producers in their neighborhood.

## Open the prototype
- Hosted link: see the link provided with the submission.
- Locally: open `Prototype.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No install and no internet connection needed.

## Product categories (demo catalog: 22 products, 6 stores)
1. Textiles & Fiber Arts: embroidery and monogrammed linen, handloom stoles, crochet and macramé, block printing and tie-dye, quilting.
2. Food & Pantry: artisanal cookies and brownies, micro-bakery sourdough, jams, chutneys and pickles, chocolates, granola and snack mixes.
3. Aroma & Self-Care: soy and beeswax candles, cold-process soaps, solid perfumes and oils, bath salts.
4. Resin, Clay & Home: resin art, polymer clay jewelry, studio pottery and terracotta, pressed flower crafts.
5. Paper & Keepsakes: handmade paper and stationery, calligraphy, portrait illustration, gift hampers.
6. Green & Eco-Crafts: terrariums and bonsai, upcycled glass and wood decor.

## Suggested review path (about 3 minutes)
1. Discover: search "candle", filter by area, tap a category pill.
2. Add products to the cart, open the cart, check out with a pickup slot.
3. Order tracking: see the new order; then Seller studio > Live order board and advance its status.
4. Seller studio > Products & stock: add or edit a product, adjust stock, see low-stock alerts (3 or fewer).
5. AI workspace > Listing helper: try "hand-embroidered handkerchief with initials" and review the suggested card.
6. AI workspace > Growth coach: rule-based tips that react to stock levels.

## Prototype limitations
Front-end only. It does not connect to Supabase, Razorpay, an AI provider or a backend. No real payment is taken. Data is stored in the browser's localStorage (reset by clearing site data). The AI helper and growth coach use simple local rules and sample figures, not a live model. Not for real customers or production orders.
